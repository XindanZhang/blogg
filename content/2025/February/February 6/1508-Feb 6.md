## Overfitting

Flexible Models: High-degree polynomials possess a strong fitting capability, enabling them to fit curves of arbitrary shapes, even passing through every point in the training data.

If a model's complexity is too high or it is over-trained, it may learn the noise in the training data as well, mistakenly believing the noise to be part of the data distribution.
